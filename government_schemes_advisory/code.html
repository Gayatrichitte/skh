Government Schemes Advisory

Tailored recommendations for your onion crop in Nashik based on current agricultural policies.

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Crop Insurance

Relevance for you

Protects your onion investment against unseasonal rains and price crashes common in the Nashik region during this season.

Key Benefits

  • Low premium for commercial crops (5%).
  • Coverage for post-harvest losses.
  • Direct settlement to bank account.

Per Drop More Crop (PDMC)

Irrigation Support

Relevance for you

Onions require consistent moisture. This scheme subsidizes drip irrigation, reducing water usage and improving bulb size.

Key Benefits

  • Subsidy up to 55% for small/marginal farmers.
  • Improves fertilizer use efficiency.
  • Reduces weed growth in onion beds.
